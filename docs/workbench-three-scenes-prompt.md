## 提示词

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: one landscape concept sheet with three equally sized horizontal panels of a premium interactive HTML Moka coffee workbench, each panel a feasible desktop browser view; no monitor frames. Input Image 1 is existing operation interface for layout continuity, Input Image 2 is approved homepage style reference. Build a creative yet restrained Italian advertising poster art direction: warm cream, copper/rust, espresso black, large expressive diagonal condensed Italian typography, plenty of breathing space. Do NOT design an ornate vintage room or generic dashboard. Three related workbench moments in ONE sheet: panel 1 'PREPARARE' preparation, bright warm limestone table, enlarged open straight octagonal aluminum lower chamber and a small realistic metal water pitcher, a compact left technical cutaway and water level control; panel 2 'FUOCO' brewing, whole upright Moka on a low dark enamel single burner, warm window light and tasteful copper geometric poster wall, compact left sectional view showing liquid water forced up the funnel through the coffee bed into upper chamber, right low-profile heat knob and small current-flow feedback; panel 3 'SERVIRE' serve/mix, hero transparent drink glass on white saucer with visible black coffee or milk coffee, small Moka placed behind on cork heat mat, milk pitcher, a paper recipe slip, brighter window-side counter. Real-time 3D feasibility: simple plane table, flat wall poster planes, one shared soft directional window light, plausible bounded camera cuts, modeled metal/plastic/porcelain/glass, minimal static props. Refine aluminum to fine satin cast grain with polished screw collars, matte black bakelite handle, brass valve; all separated Moka parts share same assembly axis. Every interactive control must look like simple HTML/SVG, not invisible magical gestures. Keep labels sparse and precise, large Italian short labels only; no unreadable paragraphs, no complex machine UI, no fake slider rendered inside a photograph. Composition: restrained 3D product scene at center-right of each panel, neutral cream left workspace with small legible diagram and lots of whitespace. Consistent connected light, sophisticated warm Italian poster design rather than Chinese 1980s nostalgia. No people, no new modes or tutorial entrance, no decorative clutter. Show distinct camera framing and ambient context in three scenes while preserving the same visual family.
